5th-5M Portal - Premium UI & CR Management Update

What was added without removing existing functionality:
- Premium responsive UI polish across the portal.
- CR-managed portal logo: upload an image or paste a logo URL in CR Portal > Settings. The same logo appears in Student Portal.
- Today and Tomorrow timetable preview on Student Portal.
- CR Timetable Manager has quick Today/Tomorrow controls; the existing full-week timetable management remains unchanged.
- Student Feedback manager in CR Portal for discussion posts and reported issues, including issue status management and removal.
- Existing Class Hub, polls/vote records, assignments, exams, study materials, photo slider, attendance, reports, leaderboard and student tools remain available.

Database note:
The Settings table now has a nullable portalLogoUrl field. After deploying this version, run:
  npx prisma db push
